NOTES ANNEXED TO BALANCE SHEET AND FORMING PART OF ACCOUNTS

  • (A). Significant Accounting Policies

      • (i) Basis of Accounting: The financial statements have been prepared on historical cost convention and on cash basis of accounting, considering the nature and objectives of the activities of the Village Organization (VO).
      (ii) Fixed Assets: Fixed assets are stated at cost of acquisition / installation including incidental expenses relating thereto. No depreciation has been provided on fixed assets.

  • (B) Notes on Accounts:

      • (i) Direct Benefit Transfers: Certain scheme funds / benefits have been transferred directly to the accounts of SHG,s or members without routing through the bank account of the VO and have been considered based on information provided by the VO.

      (ii) Start-up Fund: Start-up Fund received by the VO for meeting initial operational and administrative requirements has been treated as income and recognized in the Income & Expenditure Account. Expenditure incurred out of the fund has been accounted for on actual basis. Capital expenditure has been capitalized, whereas revenue expenditure has been charged to the Income & Expenditure Account under respective heads.

      (iii) Vulnerability Reduction Fund (VRF): If VRF received by the VO for providing financial assistance to vulnerable members has been accounted for separately wherever proper records were maintained. In absence of proper records, related disbursements and receivables have been considered along with CIF loan transactions, subject to reconciliation and verification.

      (iv) MKSP & CMSA Tool Bank Fund: If Funds received by the VO under MKSP & CMSA for Tool Bank purposes have been utilized for procurement of agricultural tools and equipment. The utilized amount has been reflected under Fixed Assets.

      (v) Pass-through Receipts & Payments: Receipts and payments such as CRP / Cadre remuneration, SVEP principal and interest, etc., received by the VO and subsequently transferred or disbursed further, have not been considered in the Income & Expenditure Account. Such transactions have been directly reflected in the Balance Sheet as receivable or payable, as the case may be.

      (vi) Contingent liabilities: Contingent liabilities are reported as NIL.

      (vii) Events Occurring After Balance Sheet Date: No material events having financial impact on the financial statements have occurred after the Balance Sheet date.

      (viii) Cash & Bank Balances: Cash and bank balances are subject to reconciliation and confirmation, wherever applicable.

      (ix) Loan Balances: Balances relating to CIF, VRF and other scheme loans are subject to confirmation and reconciliation.

      (x) Grants & Scheme Funds: Grants and scheme-based funds have been accounted for on the basis of records and utilization details made available by the VO.

      (xii) Unverified Balances: Certain balances appearing under receivables, payables, advances and member loan accounts are subject to reconciliation and verification.

      • (i) The audit has been conducted on the basis of books of accounts, records, and information produced before us by the Accountant and office bearers of the VO.
