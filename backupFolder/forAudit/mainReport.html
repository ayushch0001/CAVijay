(A) PRINCIPAL ACCOUNTING POLICIES :

(i) Basis of Accounting :

The financial statements are prepared on historical cost convention. Keeping in view the objectives and nature of activities the CLF has followed cash system of accounting.

(ii) Fixed Assets :

Fixed assets are stated at cost, which includes all expenses incidental to acquisition / installation and no depreciation is charged on the fixed assets.

(B) Notes on Accounts :

(i) Contingent liabilities: Contingent liabilities are reported as NIL.

(ii) There are no such events occurring after balance sheet date if any which are a material value.

(iii) Amount shown in Receipt and Payment accounts - Other funds/ grants received with specific purpose directions about its use: Consist of for training receipt, RBK Mandey Budget, CRP / ICRP Mandey Budget, AKM and Pashu Sakhi Mandey Budget, MKSP Tool bank Budget, PG Group Fund, Krishi Saman Related Budget etc. and some Other funds / grants for specific purpose.

(iv) Other Income and Other Expenses also includes livelihood related income and Expenses for the year.

(C) Comments:

(i) There were some idle funds in the last 3 months of the year ended on 31.03.20{{year}}. Amounts are in the specific points reporting point no (f).

(ii) Our Report is based on the basis of books, records and information provided to us by the PRP, Accountants and office bearers of the cluster.
